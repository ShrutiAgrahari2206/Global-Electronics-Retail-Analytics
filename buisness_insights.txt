# Global Electronics Retail Business Analytics

## Project Objective

Analyze global electronics retail sales data to understand revenue, profitability, customer distribution, 
product performance, and store performance using Excel and Power BI.

## Dataset

Source: Maven Analytics – Global Electronics Retailer dataset

The dataset contains information about customers, products, sales transactions, stores, and exchange rates.

### Main Tables

- Customers – customer demographics and location details
- Products – product, brand, category, cost, and price information
- Sales – orders, dates, customers, products, quantities, and currencies
- Stores – store location, size, and opening-date information
- Exchange Rates – daily currency exchange rates

## Tools Used

- Excel – Data cleaning and validation
- Power BI – Data modeling, DAX calculations, and dashboard development
- DAX – Business metrics and KPI calculations

## Data Cleaning & Preparation

The raw datasets were cleaned and validated in Excel before being imported into Power BI.

Key cleaning steps included:

- Removed unnecessary leading and trailing spaces from text fields.
- Checked and handled blank values.
- Checked duplicate records and key columns for uniqueness.
- Converted date fields into proper date format.
- Converted numeric fields such as cost, price, quantity, and exchange rates into numeric data types.
- Validated product prices and costs for consistency.
- Checked sales quantities to ensure they were positive.
- Validated currency codes and key fields.
- Retained valid blank values where they represented meaningful business cases, such as delivery dates for 
  undelivered orders and store area for the online store.
- Created matching date-currency keys for connecting sales transactions with exchange-rate data.

## Power BI Data Model

The cleaned datasets were imported into Power BI and connected using relationships between primary and 
foreign keys.

### Relationships

- Customers → Sales: CustomerKey
- Products → Sales: ProductKey
- Stores → Sales: StoreKey
- Exchange Rates → Sales: Date + Currency
- Date Table → Sales: Order Date

The data model was designed to support accurate filtering and analysis across customers, products, 
stores, dates, and currencies.

## DAX Measures

The following DAX measures were created in Power BI to calculate key business metrics:

- Total Revenue – Calculates total sales revenue using quantity sold and product unit price.
- Total Cost – Calculates total product cost using quantity sold and product unit cost.
- Total Profit – Calculates revenue minus total cost.
- Profit Margin % – Calculates profit as a percentage of revenue.
- Total Units Sold – Calculates the total quantity of products sold.
- Total Orders – Counts unique sales orders.
- Average Order Value – Calculates average revenue per order.
- Total Customers – Counts unique customers who placed orders.
- Revenue per Square Meter – Calculates revenue generated per square meter of store space.

## Business Questions

1. Which product categories and subcategories generate the most revenue and profit?
2. Which brands contribute the most revenue?
3. Which countries and states generate the highest revenue?
4. How are customers distributed across countries?
5. How does store size relate to revenue?
6. What business actions can be derived from the analysis?




1. Category Performance
Computers is the highest-revenue category and also generates the highest profit among all product categories.
2. Subcategory Performance
Desktops generate the highest profit among the product subcategories analyzed.
3. Brand Performance
Adventure Works generates the highest revenue among the brands analyzed.
4. Country Performance
The United States generates the highest revenue among the countries analyzed.
5. State Performance
California generates the highest revenue among the states analyzed.
6. Customer Distribution
The United States accounts for the largest share of customers, followed by the United Kingdom and Canada.
7. Store Size & Revenue
Larger stores generally tend to generate higher revenue, although the relationship is not perfectly 
consistent across all stores.

Business Recommendations:-

1. Focus on Computers
Since Computers contribute the highest revenue and profit, the business should closely monitor inventory, 
pricing, and sales performance within this category.
2. Monitor Desktop Profitability
Since Desktops generate the highest profit among subcategories, the business should closely track their
sales volume, pricing, costs, and inventory availability.
3. Monitor the U.S. Market
The U.S. is the largest revenue-generating and customer-contributing market, so the business should 
closely monitor U.S. sales, customer demand, and regional performance.
4. Monitor State-Level Performance
California generates the highest revenue among the states analyzed, so the business should monitor 
its sales trends, customer demand, and product performance at the state level.
5. Evaluate Store Performance Beyond Size
Since larger stores generally generate higher revenue but store size alone does not explain performance, 
the business should evaluate revenue efficiency alongside store size.
6. Monitor High-Revenue Brands
Adventure Works generates the highest revenue among the brands analyzed, so the business should monitor 
its sales trends, product demand, and contribution to overall revenue.
